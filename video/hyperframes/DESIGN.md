# Creative Pic — product film

## Style Prompt
An airy, professional studio film matching the landing page: warm ivory, translucent surfaces, ink typography, and restrained mauve accents. Start with a deliberate brand reveal, show genuine project screenshots, and end on a clear download invitation. Borrow the reference site's generous scale and product-first storytelling, while keeping Creative Pic's own copy and identity.

## Colors
- #f6f5f2 — warm canvas
- #19181b — primary ink
- #6c6871 — secondary copy
- #8a749d — mauve accent
- #e6deec — glass surface

## Typography
Arial for clear product statements; Courier New for precise version, platform, and scene labels. Headlines 82–112px; supporting copy 28–34px; metadata 22px. Distinguish statements from labels through scale and spacing.

## Motion
Measured entrances, 0.6–0.9s easing; gentle screenshot push-ins; consistent half-second crossfades. First entrance at 0.2s. No decorative loops. Every frame is deterministic. Screenshots remain legible and are never passed off as a live screen recording.

## What NOT to Do
- No invented product capabilities, adoption numbers, or testimonials.
- No imitation OpenDesign branding or copied marketing copy.
- No rapid flashes, random movement, or excessive camera moves.
- No loud gradients behind small type or unreadable screenshot crops.
- No voiceover or soundtrack; the film is intentionally silent.
